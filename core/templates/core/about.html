About EventHub

We started EventHub because finding things to do shouldn't feel like a second job. Endless listings, no sense of what's actually worth your evening, and zero connection to what you're into — we built something better.

Our mission

Nepal has an incredible, fast-growing events scene — concerts, festivals, workshops, community gatherings — but discovering the right one has always meant scrolling through everything and hoping something sticks. EventHub exists to close that gap: a platform that actually learns what you care about, instead of showing everyone the same list.

How it works

Every time you browse, save, or book an event, EventHub quietly learns a little more about what you're into. That feeds into recommendations you can actually trust — and every suggestion comes with a reason, so it never feels like a black box. Pair that with real-time trending (based on what's genuinely picking up momentum right now, not stale view counts from months ago), and you've got a platform that gets smarter the more you use it.

Built for organizers too

EventHub isn't just for people looking for their next night out. Organizers get real visibility into how their events are performing — views, registrations, and a popularity signal that helps them plan with more confidence, not guesswork.

What we stand for
  • Transparency — you'll always know why we're showing you something.
  • Local-first — pricing in NPR, built around how events actually happen in Nepal.
  • Always improving — the more EventHub is used, the better it gets.

We're just getting started. Thanks for being part of it.
